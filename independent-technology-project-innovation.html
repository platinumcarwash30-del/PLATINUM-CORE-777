INDEPENDENT TECHNOLOGY PROJECTPLATINUM CORE 777 / JOURNAL

Independent technology and innovation from Belgrade.

PLATINUM CORE 777 is an independently developed technology project that began with a real business in Belgrade and is being developed toward a wider digital system for businesses and people.

From a real business to a wider idea

The project began under the pressure of real work, limited resources and the need to solve practical problems. That experience became the foundation for a larger idea: a modular core that can connect business tools, people, companies and trusted processes.

Innovation does not always begin in a large laboratory. It can begin with a person who sees a recurring problem and keeps working toward a better structure.

The modular core behind the project

PLATINUM CORE 777 is being developed as a connected ecosystem of applications and services. The concept includes business software, licensed websites and applications, digital organization, Core Review, business discovery and controlled participation.

The modular approach allows the system to grow step by step while keeping its central idea understandable: one connected core for different business and human needs.

Core Review and public testing

Core Review is a user-facing part of the project focused on people, businesses, feedback and responsible discovery. It is entering public testing while the wider system continues through development, documentation and technical verification.

Testing is important because a serious system must be checked in real conditions, with real users and clear feedback before it expands.

The second phase and the road ahead

The project is currently in its second development phase. The next stage includes continued testing, improved documentation, stronger connections between modules and the gradual preparation of licensing and public services.

Independent innovation needs time, equipment, feedback and support. People who want to follow the journey can read the Journal, explore Core Review or contact Marko directly.
