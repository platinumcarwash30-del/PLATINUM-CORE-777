CORE REVIEWACCOUNT CONTROL

Delete your
account.

Effective date: 26 September 2026
This page explains how to request deletion of a Core Review account and the personal data associated with it.

1. Delete from the app

  1. Open Core Review and sign in.
  2. Open Settings.
  3. Select Request profile deletion and submit the request.
  4. Follow any confirmation shown in the app.

2. Request deletion online

If you cannot access the app, email contact@platinumcore777.com with the subject Core Review account deletion request.

Include the email address used for the account. Never send your password.

3. What will be deleted

After the request is verified, we delete the account profile and personal data associated with the account, including submitted profile information and user-submitted activity where applicable.

4. Limited retention

Some information may be retained only when necessary for legal obligations, fraud prevention, security, dispute resolution or audit records. Where retention is required, access is restricted and the information is removed when the retention reason ends. We aim to process verified requests within 30 days.

5. More information

For details about information handled by Core Review, read the Privacy Policy. Core Review is developed by Marko Ćuća as part of PLATINUM CORE 777.
