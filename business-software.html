BUSINESS SOFTWAREPLATINUM CORE 777

A closed software core for businesses, entrepreneurs and people.

PLATINUM CORE 777 is an independent, modular software system from Belgrade that provides licensed digital services, custom websites, tailored applications, document organization and a connected user experience through Core Review.

What is PLATINUM CORE 777?

PLATINUM CORE 777 is a closed and controlled software core for companies, entrepreneurs and individuals who need a structured digital foundation for their own work and needs. It is not only one application. It is a connected system of services built around licensing, organization and long-term digital support.

What does a license include?

A license may include a website, an application developed around the user’s requirements, 24-hour system availability, storage of important documents, organized digital archiving and connection with accounting or other necessary services. Three service packages are planned and will be presented separately after the licensing model is introduced.

Core Review for licensed businesses

Core Review is the user-facing application within the PLATINUM CORE 777 system. It is intended exclusively for users and customers of businesses licensed by PLATINUM CORE 777. Users can leave feedback after visiting a licensed business, discover the available database of trusted businesses and connect through communities, groups and events.

Why the system is different

PLATINUM CORE 777 connects the licensed business, its digital services and its users inside one controlled core. The project is designed to reduce unreliable business presentation, encourage real user feedback and create clearer relationships between people, businesses and services.

Control, trust and accountability

Users, licensed businesses, reviews and communities are governed by platform rules. Misleading activity, manipulation, abuse or other serious violations may lead to review, restriction, suspension or removal from the system. The aim is to create a digital environment where participation carries responsibility.

Who is developing it?

PLATINUM CORE 777 and Core Review are currently developed independently by Marko Ćuća, Author, Founder & CEO. He is currently the only person directly responsible for the project’s development, organization, direction and next stage.

Second development phase

The project is currently in its second development phase. The first stage established the core concept, system foundation and connected architecture. The current stage focuses on integration, testing, public preparation and the release of the Core Review test version.

Built in Belgrade, prepared for the world

PLATINUM CORE 777 is being built from Belgrade with a long-term ambition to connect licensed businesses, users and digital services across wider markets. The system is still developing, but its foundation, direction and purpose are already defined.
