CORE REVIEWSAFETY

Child Safety
Standards.

Effective date: 26 September 2026
These standards apply to Core Review, the user-facing application developed as part of PLATINUM CORE 777. We maintain a zero-tolerance policy toward child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and child sexual abuse material (CSAM).

1. Zero tolerance

Core Review prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation, child sexual abuse material, grooming, sexualization of minors, sextortion, trafficking, solicitation and any conduct that harms, exploits or endangers a child. This prohibition applies to every part of the application, including profiles, reviews, messages, communities, uploaded material and links.

2. Who these standards cover

These standards apply to every person who uses Core Review, including users, business representatives, moderators, owners and other participants. They apply regardless of whether content is public, private, direct, temporary or shared inside a community.

3. Reporting a concern

Users can submit safety concerns, abuse reports or policy feedback through the in-app feedback/contact channel. Reports can also be sent to the designated child-safety contact at contact@platinumcore777.com. Please include the relevant account, message, review, community or other information when it is safe to do so.

If a child is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement first.

4. Review and enforcement

Reports are reviewed according to the seriousness and credibility of the concern. We may remove content, restrict visibility, suspend or close accounts, preserve relevant information, block access and cooperate with lawful requests. We do not tolerate retaliation against a person who reports a safety concern in good faith.

5. CSAM response

Confirmed CSAM is prohibited and will be removed or restricted as appropriate. Where required by applicable law, confirmed CSAM and credible CSAE reports will be reported to the relevant regional or national authority, including the appropriate child-safety reporting authority for the jurisdiction involved.

6. Child-safety point of contact

The designated point of contact for child-safety matters is Marko Ćuća, Author, Founder & CEO of PLATINUM CORE 777. He is responsible for receiving safety notifications, coordinating review and responding to lawful requests related to CSAE or CSAM.

7. Compliance and cooperation

Core Review is intended to operate in accordance with applicable child-safety laws and regulations. We cooperate with competent authorities and trusted safety partners when legally required or when necessary to protect a child from abuse, exploitation or immediate danger.

8. Related policies

These standards work together with the Core Review Privacy Policy, the application's terms and community rules, and any safety instructions shown in the app. We may update these standards when the application, our procedures or legal requirements change.
