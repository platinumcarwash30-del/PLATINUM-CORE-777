CORE REVIEWCLIENT CONNECTION

A customer review app designed to bring people and businesses closer.

Core Review is the people-facing part of PLATINUM CORE 777, being developed to connect clients, trusted businesses and verifiable feedback.

What is Core Review?

Core Review is being developed as a controlled review and client connection experience. It is designed to give users a clear way to interact with participating businesses while helping companies receive structured, meaningful feedback.

Why verified feedback matters

Useful reviews should be connected to real visits, clear roles and responsible processes. Core Review is being built around that principle as part of a wider business software system.

Part of a larger business core

Core Review is not an isolated review page. It is connected to the wider PLATINUM CORE 777 vision: one modular system for businesses, clients and communities.

Current development stage
