Privacy
Policy.

Effective date: 26 September 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Core Review, developed as part of PLATINUM CORE 777, handles information when people use the application and related services.

1. Who is responsible?

Core Review is an independent application developed by Marko Ćuća, Author, Founder and CEO of PLATINUM CORE 777. For privacy questions, contact contact@platinumcore777.com.

2. Information we may process

Depending on the features a person uses, Core Review may process:

  • account information such as email address and display name;
  • profile information voluntarily provided by the user;
  • reviews, messages, community activity and other content submitted by the user;
  • business, membership and access information needed to operate the service;
  • basic technical, security and diagnostic information required to keep the app working.

3. How information is used

Information is used to create and secure accounts, provide Core Review features, connect users with participating businesses and communities, display user-submitted content, prevent abuse, respond to support requests, maintain system security and improve reliability.

4. User content and visibility

Information or content that a user chooses to place in a profile, review, message or community may be visible to the audience intended by that feature. Users should avoid publishing information they do not want to share. We do not sell personal information.

5. Service providers

Core Review may use trusted infrastructure and service providers for authentication, database hosting, application delivery, security, communications and diagnostics. They may process information only as needed to provide those services and under appropriate security obligations.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the service, including controlled access and secure connections where available. No internet service can guarantee absolute security, so users should also protect their account credentials and devices.

7. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only for as long as needed to provide the service, maintain security, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations or protect legitimate rights. A user may request account or personal-data assistance by contacting contact@platinumcore777.com.

8. Children

Core Review is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If a parent or guardian believes that such information was submitted, please contact us so it can be reviewed and removed where appropriate.

9. International use

Core Review may be accessed internationally. Information may be processed in the countries where PLATINUM CORE 777 or its service providers operate, subject to applicable legal and security requirements.

10. Policy updates

This policy may be updated when the app, services or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when a revised version is published.

11. Contact
